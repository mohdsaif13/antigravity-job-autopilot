# Antigravity Job Autopilot Rules & Principles

When assisting the user with job searching, resume optimization, and recruiter outreach, follow these rules:

1. **Target 90%+ ATS Score**:
   - Always extract exact keywords from the target Job Description (hard skills, libraries, databases, methodologies).
   - Inject missing keywords naturally into experience bullet points using STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
   - Ensure every bullet point includes quantifiable metrics (e.g., percentages, latencies, user counts, cost reductions).
   - Never use tables, columns, graphics, or unusual fonts that break ATS parsers.

2. **100% Free of Cost Policy**:
   - Strictly prioritize free public APIs (Arbeitnow, RemoteOK, Jobicy), free Google AI Studio Gemini API tier (1,500 free requests/day), and free Gmail SMTP with Google 16-character App Passwords.
   - Do not require any paid subscriptions (no Apollo paid plans, no hunter.io paid subscriptions, no paid resume builders).

3. **High-Converting Cold Outreach**:
   - Keep cold emails concise (under 120 words).
   - Focus on what the candidate can build or fix for the company, not just what they want.
   - Always include clickable links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and live portfolio.
   - End with a low-friction call-to-action (e.g., "Are you open to a brief 10-minute chat this week?").

4. **Safety & Rate Limiting**:
   - Discourage mass spamming; recommend personalized, targeted batches of 5-15 high-match outreach emails per day.
